<objective>
Make the three `Animator` factories refuse a Thread device by default, with a keyword-only
`enable_thread` opt-in, through one guard helper that the Conductor (plan 05) will reuse.

Purpose: ANIM-05 R1, R2 and the factory half of R8. SEED-003 recorded that a Thread mesh cannot
carry a 20 FPS pixel stream; the library currently builds an Animator against any device, so a
caller silently pushes frame traffic onto the mesh. The refusal happens at construction, names the
connectivity reason, and is overridable only at the call site.

Output: `LifxThreadAnimationError`, `src/lifx/animation/guard.py`, the three guarded factories and
their docstrings, and `tests/test_animation/test_thread_guard.py`.

**Keeping R2's "existing suite passes unmodified" provable.** Every new test goes into the new
module `tests/test_animation/test_thread_guard.py`. The existing `tests/test_animation/test_*.py`
modules are not touched by this plan, and a gate proves they are byte-identical to the
merge-base. Plan 08's PLC0415 import hoist is the first and only later change to them, and it
runs after this evidence is recorded.
</objective>
