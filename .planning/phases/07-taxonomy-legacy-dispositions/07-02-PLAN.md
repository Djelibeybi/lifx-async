<objective>
Rewrite `ThemeLibrary`'s category navigation over the app's own 9-category taxonomy:
add `get_categories()`, rewrite `get_by_category()` to read categories from the generated
records with D-09 slug-rule normalisation on both sides, give the 6 pre-v1.2 legacy names
their locked fates via the private `_LEGACY_CATEGORIES` map (2 resolve, 4 raise naming a
replacement), relocate `derive_slug()` into the package so generator and library share one
rule, and correct the two stale docstring sites in library.py — implementing D-01, D-02,
D-03, D-04 and D-11 (sites 1-2) from 07-CONTEXT.md. Closes the WR-02 defect (every
`get_by_category(theme.category)` call currently raises); issue #200 is the same defect —
per D-13 it is closed at ship, NOT by this plan.

Purpose: callers can list categories and navigate by them (META-03), and every pre-v1.2
category name either still returns themes or fails naming its replacement (META-04).
Output: rewritten library.py, new slug.py, generator import change, full behaviour tests.

**Deferred (do NOT implement):** issue #201 alias/primary enumeration (D-12),
`get_legacy_categories()` public accessor (declined, D-03), restoring sport categories
or reviving winter/romantic/dramatic (SPEC out-of-scope — they stop being silently
filtered, nothing more).

**Spelling note:** new user-facing strings use Australian English ("recognised"). The old
message at library.py:193 uses "recognized" — it is deleted with the rewrite. Do not
rewrite unrelated American spellings elsewhere in the codebase.
</objective>
