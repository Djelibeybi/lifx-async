## Artifacts this phase produces

Phase 12 adds no public API symbol, dependency, schema, generated protocol file, persistence field,
or mDNS behaviour. This plan makes `find_by_ip()` deterministically finalise its owned discovery
generator, modifies `discover_devices()` to own its private `_discover_with_packet()` delegate,
updates that delegate's family-aware transport construction, corrects one stale value-suppressed
transport lifecycle comment, updates the `find_by_ip()` and IPv6 E2E module docstrings, and adds the
test-only `_DiscoveryObservation`, `_make_observed_discovery_transport()`,
`TestIpv6TargetedDiscovery`, and `TestDiscoveryGeneratorOwnership` symbols.

## Review feedback disposition

Antigravity's broader claim names `find_by_serial()`, `discover()`, `find_by_label()`,
`Device.set_location()`, and `Device.set_group()` as additional `aclosing` candidates. The executor
MUST NOT expand Phase 12 into those callers: `find_by_serial()` and merged default discovery are
Phase 13 work, `find_by_label()` is explicitly unchanged by the milestone, and the two device
metadata setters are unrelated to FIND-06. This plan accepts the source-grounded `find_by_ip()` and
`discover_devices()` ownership repair only; the broader claim is rejected for this phase because it
crosses locked boundaries, not because it is presumed false.
