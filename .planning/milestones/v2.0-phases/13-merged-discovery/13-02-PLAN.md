<interfaces>
`_discover_with_packet(packet, *, timeout, broadcast_address, port, max_response_time, idle_timeout_multiplier, _address_is_prevalidated=False)` is the sole validated UDP producer. It yields accepted `DiscoveryResponse` values only after source, packet, service, address, serial, and first-wins checks.

`DiscoveredDevice(serial, ip, port, response_time, timeout, max_retries)` embeds subscriber-specific request settings. Those objects must be constructed after fan-out, not stored in shared state.

The compatibility key is exactly `(broadcast_address, port, timeout, max_response_time, idle_timeout_multiplier)`. `device_timeout` and `max_retries` are excluded.
</interfaces>

## Review feedback dispositions

- Preserve the D-10/D-11 process-wide coordinator. The proposed same-loop-only redesign is rejected because it would permit one compatible wire sweep per caller loop and violate the locked cross-loop load contract.
- Accept the caller-owned overall wall deadline refinement: it starts at each public subscription and bounds prefix replay, device construction, and yield, while the active producer retains its original wire deadline and is never extended. This preserves FIND-02's public timeout upper bound and D-10's compatible sharing contract.
- Reject the proposed fresh-sweep remaining-window threshold because it would split compatible overlapping callers and conflicts with locked D-10 unless CONTEXT.md is changed by the user.
- Reject the claim that a caller-loop mDNS `ContextVar` automatically solves UDP observation across the process-wide coordinator. The subscription must carry the caller-owned sink explicitly across the coordinator thread/loop boundary and scope events to that measured call.
- Add bounded shutdown-of-last-resort, post-fork reset, and abandoned-subscriber detachment. Do not install signal handlers; the coordinator does not receive `KeyboardInterrupt` directly.
