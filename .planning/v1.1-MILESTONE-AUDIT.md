Discovery deliberately cannot reuse `_transmit_and_listen`: that engine stops
retransmitting after the first yield (`connection.py:576`), which would defeat DISC-01's
requirement to keep re-broadcasting after early responders answer. The separation is a
design outcome, and both sides carry traceability comments.

### Warning (non-blocking)

`api.discover()` awaits `create_device()` inside the discovery loop (`src/lifx/api.py:792`).
Consumer time counts against the idle window (documented at `discovery.py:190-194`), and
`create_device()` issues Phase 3 requests with a 16 s wall deadline (`const.py:42`) against
a ~4 s idle window. A device that answers `GetService` and then dies can suspend the
generator long enough for `deadline.idle_expired` to fire before queued responses from
later re-broadcasts are read, silently truncating the sweep.

`discover_devices()` itself is sound — the hazard lives only in the convenience wrapper.
Candidate fixes: buffer serials and construct devices after the generator completes, mark
the response after the yield returns, or raise the effective idle multiplier inside
`discover()`.
